Wishlist Wheel

Paste your wishlist items, spin the wheel, and see what lands under the pointer.

Import from Amazon
  1. Set your wishlist to Public or Shared.
  2. In a desktop browser, create a new bookmark and paste the bookmarklet below as its URL.
  3. Open your wishlist on amazon.com and click that bookmark. It scrolls to load every item, then copies the JSON. Wait for the confirmation alert.
  4. Come back here and press Paste JSON.

If the alert says no items were found, Amazon has changed its page markup and the selectors need updating.
